# Abstract Template B: Challenge, Insight, Contribution

Choose this sequence when the reasoning behind the method is needed to understand the contribution.

1. Establish the task and operating conditions.
2. State the unresolved difficulty.
3. Explain the observation or reasoning that suggests a solution.
4. Describe the mechanism that implements that reasoning.
5. Connect the mechanism to the intended benefit.
6. State the verified result.

The local notes use Neural Body to illustrate this chain: sparse camera observations create an estimation difficulty; combining evidence across frames supplies the conceptual response; a shared representation tied to moving geometry implements that response. The writing lesson is to distinguish the insight from the representation used to realize it.

A second example in the notes concerns depth-aware diffusion guidance. Its useful structure is a progression from a missing control capability to an internal signal and then to a guidance procedure. The notes do not provide a complete paper citation for this example.

```latex
% Obstacle: [what available methods cannot resolve in this setting]
% Insight: [reason a different source of information could help]
% Implementation: [specific mechanism that uses that information]
% Evidence: [observed improvement and the conditions tested]
```
