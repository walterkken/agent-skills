# Decomposing Challenges for a New Task

Turn a broad statement of difficulty into distinct requirements.

The local single-image example distinguishes limited observations, uncontrolled appearance variation, and a distributional target. The purpose is to separate sources of difficulty that call for different responses.

| Question | What to establish | What the manuscript should explain |
|---|---|---|
| Is the evidence sufficient? | Available observations and missing information | Why multiple solutions remain plausible |
| Which factors are unknown? | Variation in pose, lighting, or other relevant conditions | Why an existing assumption cannot simply be reused |
| What kind of output is needed? | A single estimate, a set, or a distribution | How the method and evaluation represent that requirement |

Use only rows that fit the actual research question. Follow every challenge with the mechanism or evidence needed to address it. Do not add artificial challenges to enlarge the apparent scope.
