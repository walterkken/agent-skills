# Example Bank Index

Use this folder for writing patterns and structural reading notes. Public-copy examples summarize the teaching points and use fill-in templates in place of extended article quotations.

## Provenance and use

The [skill README](../../README.md) already identifies Prof. Peng Sida's [research-writing notes](https://pengsida.notion.site/c1a22465a0fa4b15a12985223916048e) and [learning_research repository](https://github.com/pengsida/learning_research) as teaching sources. These links are retained from the installed skill; they have not been replaced with invented paper citations.

Names such as Neural Body, Deep Snake, ManhattanSDF, and Instant-NGP identify examples present in the local notes. Some notes do not include full article references. Consult the original publications before reusing scientific claims. A link to this teaching bank is not a substitute for a manuscript citation to the relevant research.

## Usage

1. Pick one template from a section guide.
2. Open the matching examples file.
3. Reuse the sentence logic, not exact wording.
4. Record verified primary-source citations separately when using scientific claims in a manuscript.
